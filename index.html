Research & Projects

Cross-Abstraction-Level Agentic Kernel Programming System

Core developer · June 2026–present

An LLM-driven system for optimizing kernels across TileLang, HIP C++, and AscendC on AMD GPUs and Ascend NPUs. Runtime feedback guides performance diagnosis, abstraction-level selection, and candidate rollback.

Under review

Undergraduate thesis, HUST · Dec. 2025–May 2026 · Advisor: Prof. Yunhe Zhang
